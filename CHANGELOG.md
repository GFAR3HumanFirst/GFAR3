GFAR³ should change transparently.

This record identifies meaningful public changes to the framework, testing process, and public materials. Changes should document not only what changed, but why.

## Changelog rule for future revisions

For every meaningful public change, record:

1. **What changed?** The specific wording, principle, question, safeguard, process, or documentation.
2. **Why?** The reason for the change.
3. **What prompted it?** Testing, criticism, evidence, ambiguity, or a discovered failure mode.
4. **What was deliberately not changed?** Preserve the boundary against silent drift.
5. **What remains unresolved?** Preserve uncertainty, dissent, and unanswered questions.

Do not silently overwrite prior public versions.
